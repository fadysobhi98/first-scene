The scene behind First Scene

Who we are

A Cairo-based crew of photographers and filmmakers working across Egypt and Saudi Arabia. We shoot conferences, brand launches, real estate, sports and the occasional perfect wedding dance. First Scene is Fady Sobhy and a team that treats every brief like opening night.

What we do

Full event coverage, commercial photography, promo films, interviews, reels and aftermovies. From a 3,000-seat ballroom to a two-person product shoot, we deliver edited photos and films that are ready to publish.

How we work

You tell us the occasion, we plan the shots. We arrive early, stay invisible, and catch the moments you did not see. Delivery is a view-only Drive album - fast, organized, yours to share.
